EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
===========================================================================

This report documents scheduled and corrective maintenance activities
performed during the period 2024-01-10 to 2024-01-15 at the Westfield
Industrial Plant. Four maintenance interventions were completed, two of
which were emergency call-outs triggered by sensor alerts. All critical
systems have been returned to operational status.

===========================================================================
1. BOILER ROOM A — HEAT EXCHANGER INSPECTION (Planned)
===========================================================================

Work Order:     WO-2024-0088
Equipment:      Shell-and-tube heat exchanger HX-101
Location:       Boiler Room A
Date/Time:      2024-01-10, 08:00–14:30
Technicians:    M. Santos, R. Patel

Findings:
- Scale build-up detected on tube surfaces (estimated 3 mm thickness)
  on the hot-side inlet section. Scale type: calcium carbonate.
- Overall heat transfer coefficient reduced by approximately 18% compared
  to last inspection baseline (October 2023).
- Shell-side pressure drop: 0.38 bar (acceptable limit: 0.50 bar).
- No tube leaks detected during hydrostatic pressure test at 8.5 bar.

Actions taken:
- Chemical descaling performed using 10% citric acid solution. Circulation
  time: 4 hours. pH monitored continuously; neutralised to pH 7.0 before drain.
- Post-cleaning efficiency test confirms heat transfer coefficient restored
  to within 5% of baseline.
- Temperature sensor TEMP-001 calibration verified: offset = +0.3°C applied.

Recommendations:
- Reduce water treatment dosing interval from monthly to bi-weekly to
  prevent future scale build-up.
- Schedule next inspection in 6 months (target: 2024-07-10).

2. COOLANT LINE 1 — PUMP BEARING FAILURE (Emergency)
===========================================================================

Work Order:     WO-2024-0089
Equipment:      Centrifugal pump P-204, Coolant Line 1
Location:       Pump Station 2
Date/Time:      2024-01-14, 22:15–2024-01-15, 03:40
Technicians:    A. Kowalski (on-call), D. Fletcher

Trigger:
Sensor VIB-006 reported vibration levels exceeding 1.5 mm/s at 22:15 on
2024-01-14. Simultaneously, flow sensor FLOW-004 began registering a
declining flow rate on Coolant Line 1 (from nominal 12.4 L/min to 4.1 L/min
by 01:00 on 2024-01-15). Alert threshold: 5.0 L/min.

Root cause:
Inspection found premature failure of the drive-end roller bearing (SKF
6310-2RS1). Bearing inner race showed spalling consistent with water
ingress. Seal failure identified as contributing factor — lip seal on
shaft had hardened and cracked, allowing coolant to contaminate the
bearing grease.

Actions taken:
- Pump P-204 isolated and de-energised under LOTO procedure.
- Defective bearing replaced with SKF 6310-2RS1/C3 (updated clearance
  specification for high-moisture environments).
- Shaft lip seal replaced with PTFE-lined variant (part #SL-204-PTFE).
- Bearing housing cleaned and repacked with water-resistant grease
  (Mobil Polyrex EM).
- Pump re-commissioned at 07:00 on 2024-01-15. Flow rate restored to
  12.4 L/min. Vibration reading post-repair: 0.43 mm/s (within normal).

Parts used:
- 1× SKF 6310-2RS1/C3 bearing (stock item)
- 1× Lip seal SL-204-PTFE (ordered from warehouse)
- 0.5 kg Mobil Polyrex EM grease

Estimated downtime cost: ~£3,200 (12 hours partial capacity reduction on
Coolant Line 1).

Recommendations:
- Replace all same-type seals on pumps P-205 and P-206 proactively during
  next scheduled maintenance window.
- Install moisture detector in pump housing P-204, P-205, P-206.
- Increase vibration monitoring frequency for VIB-006 from 10-minute to
  5-minute intervals for the next 30 days.

3. STORAGE TANK B — HUMIDITY INGRESS INVESTIGATION (Emergency)
===========================================================================

Work Order:     WO-2024-0090
Equipment:      Storage Tank B, humidity control system
Location:       Storage Tank B, Section 4
Date/Time:      2024-01-15, 01:00–06:30
Technicians:    P. Nguyen, L. Osei

Trigger:
Sensor HUM-003 recorded humidity exceeding 70% at 00:30 on 2024-01-15.
Normal operating range: 40–60%. Critical threshold: 70%.

Root cause:
Roof panel joint sealant on Tank B had degraded (UV exposure over 3+
years). Overnight rain ingress raised internal humidity rapidly. Secondary
finding: dehumidifier DEHUM-B1 was non-operational due to a failed
compressor capacitor (failed silently — no alert generated).

Actions taken:
- Emergency application of polyurethane roof sealant to affected joint
  (approx. 4 m linear). Temporary tarpaulin deployed pending full repair.
- Compressor capacitor on DEHUM-B1 replaced (20 μF, 450 V AC unit).
  Dehumidifier tested and returned to service at 03:15.
- Tank B humidity returned to 47% by 06:30.

Recommendations:
- Schedule full roof resealing for Tank B in Q2 2024.
- Add monitoring alert for DEHUM-B1 operational status (current gap in
  SCADA integration).
- Review inspection schedule for all tank roof sealants — current interval
  is 5 years, recommend reducing to 3 years.

Work Order:     WO-2024-0091
Equipment:      Pressure relief valve PRV-303, Pipeline Section 3
Location:       Pipeline Section 3, Junction Box J-12
Date/Time:      2024-01-12, 09:00–11:30

- Test results logged in maintenance management system (MMS ref: T-PRV-303-2024).

Next scheduled test: 2025-01-12 (annual interval per BS EN ISO 4126-1).
